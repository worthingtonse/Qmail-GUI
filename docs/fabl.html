QMail card stack — technique demo (fabl)

One list, three modes. Broken reproduces today's bug (flex-shrink squash + transparent tints + no z-index). Fixed is Phase 1+2: opaque gradient cards, lit top edge, honest z-index, draw-a-card hover. Fanned stack is Phase 3: deliberate constant overlap exposing each card's header spine — the visible slice is the clickable slice. Click cards and watch the log verify hit-target honesty.

What to look for

Broken mode

Rows compress to min-height: 4.5rem while the 72px cartouche keeps painting — cards pile up. Click where a cartouche visibly is: the row below often gets selected, and the selected wash is color-mix(…, transparent), so the card "vanishes".

Fixed mode

flex-shrink: 0 restores geometry. Each card is an opaque gradient — lighter top edge, darker foot — with a real shadow. Hover lifts the card and raises z-index, so pointer ownership always matches the eye. Selection is an opaque accent blend: never transparent.

Fanned stack mode

Constant negative margin-top exposes the header spine of every card, like file-folder tabs. Older cards sit on top (each card tucks under the one below), so sender and subject stay readable on every card — and that's just natural DOM paint order, no z-index math. Hovering pulls the card fully out of the deck. Because hit-testing follows paint order, the strip you can see is the strip you click — misclicks are impossible by construction. Cartouche boxes are tier-colored: bit, byte, kilo, mega, giga, epic — danger → very safe — each holding the user's two symbols.
