Reading pane redesign — hero cartouche, metadata header, subject in body

Three states below: an inbox message you replied to, one still awaiting your reply, and a sent message with To/CC/BCC arrays.

Design decisions

  • Cartouche is the hero — 112px, tier-colored border with a soft glow, tier name tag beneath. It's the sender's identity mark, so it gets the visual weight.
  • Header = metadata only. From, To/CC/BCC, date, status. The subject moves into the message pane below, styled like a document title — that's where reading starts.
  • Recipient arrays as chips. Each recipient is a pill with a tier-colored dot (danger→safe at a glance). Long lists truncate to +N more, click to expand. "You" is highlighted.
  • Replied at a glance, twice. A green ↩ badge on the cartouche corner (glanceable) and an explicit chip with the reply date (precise). Unanswered inbox mail shows an amber "Awaiting your reply" chip — a gentle to-do cue.
  • BCC only where it exists — sent items show it; the grid simply grows a row.
  • Action rail stays right, but grouped: reply/reply-all/forward on top, Previous|Next as one compact segmented control at the bottom.
