================================================================================
THE THREE FONT-SIZE LITERALS — SUGGESTED DISPOSITION
================================================================================

These three violations land at the same visual size class (~9-10px),
which is below the smallest current font-size token. The pattern across
the three sites is the same: an uppercase pill / badge with letter-spacing
0.04em-0.06em, used as a tag/badge inline label.

Three options, pick one during the rename pass:

  a) Add --font-size-2xs: 0.625rem to tokens.css (one new token, used
     by 3 sites), and round EmailListPane's 0.5625rem to 0.625rem for
     consistency. Fewest tokens, slight visual drift.

  b) Add both --font-size-2xs (0.625rem) and --font-size-3xs (0.5625rem).
     Two new tokens, no visual drift, but plan §13.7 cautions against
     proliferation.

  c) Keep the literals with an inline `/* badge-only; below --font-size-xs */`
     comment as an explicit opt-out. Zero new tokens, but the gate fails
     on this file until the cap rule is loosened.

My read: (a) is the right call. The 0.0625rem (1px) round-up will be
invisible at a 9-10px uppercase pill, and one new token for three sites
is justifiable.
