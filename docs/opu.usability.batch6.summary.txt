### FIX-06: Forward Action
- Implemented "Forward" logic in `ComposeModal.jsx`.
- Pre-fills subject with `Fwd: ` (avoiding duplicates).
- Pre-fills body with a formatted forwarded message header and quoted content.

### FIX-07: Reply All Action
- Implemented "Reply All" logic in `ComposeModal.jsx`.
- Pre-fills `To` with the original sender.
- Pre-fills `CC` with all other original recipients (from `To` and `CC` lists).
- **Identity Filtering**: Implemented `isOwnAddress` helper to filter the user's own addresses (SN, pretty address, raw address) out of the recipient list to prevent "replying to self".
- Pre-fills subject with `Re: ` (avoiding duplicates).
- Added "Reply All" button to `ReadingPane.jsx`.
