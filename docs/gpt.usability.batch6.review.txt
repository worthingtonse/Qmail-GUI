1. Fixed the production build failure in ComposeModal.jsx by removing the duplicate
   parseEmailList declaration introduced during the composeContext refactor.
2. Hardened Reply All address handling:
   - own-address filtering now compares normalized serial number, address,
     auto_address, pretty address, email_address, and raw identity forms.
   - address tokens wrapped as "Name <address>" are normalized before comparison.
   - duplicate Cc recipients are removed case-insensitively while preserving the
     displayed value.
3. Preserved recipient fields returned by /qmail/db/messages/get:
   - getEmailById now maps to, cc, bcc, and subsubject aliases when present.
   - ComposeModal accepts to_addresses/cc_addresses/bcc_addresses aliases for
     draft and Reply All flows.
4. Made unavailable actions honest:
   - Reply All is disabled when no stored To/Cc recipient list is available.
   - QMailDashboard also guards direct Reply All calls and shows a notification
     instead of opening a misleading plain reply.
   - Forward is disabled in the pending/download view and guarded in
     QMailDashboard until the message is downloaded.
5. Added autoAddress/auto_address aliases to normalizeIdentityForUi so Reply All
   can filter every normalized identity form required by the Phase 1 ticket.
